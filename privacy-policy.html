Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information across Radiantt Esthetics and Radiantt Academy.

Last updated: July 6, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Dr. Debasish Biswas, operating Radiantt Esthetics (clinic) and Radiantt Academy (training programmes), collects, uses, and safeguards information when you visit this website or submit an enquiry, booking, or application form. By using this website, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you voluntarily submit it through a form on this site:

  • Name, phone number, and email address
  • Treatment, programme, or enquiry type you're interested in
  • Preferred appointment date (for clinic bookings)
  • Medical or academic qualification (for academy applications)
  • Any message, question, or health-related concern you choose to share

We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising, and we do not collect payment information through this website.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through our forms is used solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry, consultation request, or academy application
  • Schedule and confirm appointments or training sessions
  • Provide information you have specifically requested

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com) to display typography correctly. Loading these fonts may involve your browser making a request to Google's servers. No other third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts are used on this site.

5. Data Storage & Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect any information you share with us. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information you have submitted to us by contacting us using the details below. We will respond to reasonable requests within a reasonable timeframe.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults seeking aesthetic treatments or professional medical training. This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from individuals under 18.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when it was last revised.
