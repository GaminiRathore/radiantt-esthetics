Dr. Debasish Biswas is a Board Certified Aesthetic Physician with international training spanning the United States, New Delhi, and Mumbai. His dual commitment — clinical excellence at Radiantt Esthetics and education at Radiantt Academy — reflects a lifelong dedication to advancing aesthetic medicine.

Every consultation is guided by an unwavering belief in natural results, evidence-based protocols, and the highest standards of patient care.

  • Board Certified Aesthetic Physician — American Academy of Aesthetic Medicine
  • MBBS — NRS Medical College & Hospital
  • Diploma in Aesthetic Medicine — AAAM, United States
  • Fellowship in Aesthetic Medicine — New Delhi
  • Diploma in Trichology — Mumbai
