Questions Answered

Academy FAQ

Who is eligible to enroll? +
Radiantt Academy is open to Medical Doctors (MBBS, BDS, BAMS, BHMS) and Paramedics. No prior aesthetic experience is required — all skill levels are welcome.
What certification will I receive? +
You receive dual certification: one from Radiantt Academy and one from the International Education Board (IEB), Department of Aesthetic Medicine & Surgery — a globally recognised accrediting body and a mark of excellence worldwide.
Is training hands-on with real patients? +
Yes — every training day includes live, hands-on procedures with real patients. You build genuine clinical competence, not just theoretical understanding.
What support is available after the course ends? +
Post-course support includes extensive mentorship, a free observatorship at Radiantt Esthetics, clinic set-up assistance, and periodic integration sessions covering new treatments, procedures, and products.
Is the observatorship really free? +
Yes — all Radiantt Academy graduates receive a complimentary observatorship at Radiantt Esthetics after completing their programme, at absolutely no additional cost.
Do you help with clinic set-up after training? +
Yes. We guide you through setting up your own aesthetic practice — from equipment and protocols to patient acquisition and social media strategy for your clinic.
What are the course fees? +
Fees start at ₹8,599 for a Master Class (Single Topic) and go up to ₹68,999 for the Fellowship Program. All programmes include IEB certification. Call +91 98302 23384 for full details and limited seat availability.
Are seats limited? +
Yes — all batches are kept intentionally small to ensure every participant receives personalised mentorship. Limited seats are available per batch, so early enrolment is strongly recommended.
