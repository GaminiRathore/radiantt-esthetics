Botox results typically last 3–6 months. Regular maintenance sessions can extend results over time.

All treatments are performed personally by Dr. Biswas using only FDA-approved products and the strictest international safety protocols.
What should I expect at my first consultation? +
A detailed skin assessment, discussion of your aesthetic goals, medical history review, and a fully personalized treatment plan.
Is there any downtime after treatments? +
Most treatments are minimally invasive with little to no downtime. Aftercare instructions are provided and discussed in full during your consultation.
